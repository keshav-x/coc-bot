How do I activate my license key?
Activating takes less than 30 seconds:
  1. Launch ApexClash Pro on your Windows PC.
  2. Click the License tab on the left sidebar.
  3. Paste your key (starts with CAL-...) into the key field.
  4. Click Activate License. Your key is verified cryptographically via Ed25519 signature and bound to your PC immediately.
